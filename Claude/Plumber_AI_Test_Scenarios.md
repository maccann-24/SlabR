# AI Receptionist Test Scenarios — Plumbing
## 100 Questions and Scenarios for Demo Testing

---

## Category 1: Booking and Scheduling (20 Scenarios)

1. "Hi, I need to schedule a plumber to come out and look at my kitchen sink — it's been draining really slowly for about a week."
2. "Can I get someone out today? I have a leak under my bathroom vanity and it's dripping into the cabinet."
3. "What's your earliest available appointment this week? I'm pretty flexible on time."
4. "I need to book something for Saturday morning — do you work weekends?"
5. "Can I schedule a plumber for next Tuesday between 8am and noon? I need to be at work by 1."
6. "I'd like to get a quote before I book anything. Do you do free estimates?"
7. "How long does a typical service call take? I'm trying to figure out if I need to take the whole day off work."
8. "I booked an appointment last week — can I move it to Thursday instead?"
9. "I need to cancel my appointment for tomorrow. Can I reschedule for later in the month?"
10. "Do you send a reminder before the appointment? I tend to forget these things."
11. "Can I book a same-day appointment or is there a waiting list?"
12. "My landlord is supposed to handle this but he's not responding — can I book it myself and get reimbursed?"
13. "I need someone out first thing in the morning — like 7am. Is that possible?"
14. "Do you have evening appointments? I work during the day and can't take time off."
15. "How far in advance do I need to book? Is next week realistic?"
16. "I just bought a house and want a full plumbing inspection. How do I schedule that?"
17. "My tenant called me about a clogged drain — can you go directly to the property without me being there?"
18. "Can I book two things at once? I have a running toilet and a leaky outdoor faucet I want looked at together."
19. "What's the name of the plumber who'll be coming? I want to let my building's front desk know."
20. "Do I need to do anything to prepare before the plumber arrives?"

## Category 2: Emergency and Urgent Calls (15 Scenarios)

21. "My basement is flooding right now — water is coming up through the drain. I need someone immediately."
22. "I think I have a broken pipe behind my wall. I can hear water running but I don't see anything. Is this an emergency?"
23. "My water heater just started leaking all over the floor. There's probably two inches of water already."
24. "I smell gas near my water heater. Is that something you handle?"

26. "My toilet is overflowing and I can't get it to stop. What do I do right now?"
27. "A pipe burst in my kitchen and I've turned off the main water but I don't know what to do next."
28. "It's 11pm and my sump pump stopped working — we're supposed to get heavy rain tonight."
29. "I have sewage backing up into my bathtub. This just started an hour ago."
30. "My outdoor spigot snapped off and water is shooting out of the wall. I've shut off the main — how fast can someone get here?"
31. "Is there an after-hours emergency line? This can't wait until morning."
32. "How quickly can you get here for an emergency? Are we talking hours or days?"
33. "My water pressure just dropped to almost nothing in the whole house — all at once."
34. "There's water coming through my ceiling from the bathroom upstairs. I don't know where it's coming from."
35. "My kids flushed something down the toilet and now it won't flush at all and is close to overflowing."
