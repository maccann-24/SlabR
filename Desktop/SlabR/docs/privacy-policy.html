Privacy Policy

Effective: March 2026

Slabr is a local-first app. Your card data stays on your iPhone. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking.

What We Collect

Card Data

Card metadata (player, year, brand, grade, cert number, price), photos, and listing details are stored locally on your device in Core Data with FileProtectionType.complete encryption. This data never leaves your device unless you publish a listing to eBay.

Device Identifier

A random UUID is generated on first launch and stored in the iOS Keychain. It is used to scope local data. It is not linked to your identity or Apple ID.

Crash Data

In release builds, Slabr uses Sentry to collect crash reports. These include stack traces and device information (model, OS version). No personally identifiable information, card data, credentials, or images are included. Crash reporting is disabled in debug builds.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contact information
  • No browsing or search history
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Sent
PSA (api.psacard.com) Card certification lookup Cert number via HTTPS
eBay (api.ebay.com) Listing creation Card metadata, photos, pricing when you publish a listing
Sentry Crash reporting Stack traces, device info. No PII.

PSA credentials (if used) are transmitted to PSA over HTTPS and immediately cleared from memory. The resulting auth token is stored in the iOS Keychain.

eBay authentication uses ASWebAuthenticationSession (eBay-hosted login). Tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Listing data is sent to eBay only when you explicitly publish.

Data Storage

Data Location Encryption
Card data & images Core Data (on-device) FileProtectionType.complete
Auth tokens (PSA, eBay) iOS Keychain Hardware-encrypted, device-only
User preferences Core Data (on-device) FileProtectionType.complete

Slabr does not operate backend servers. There is no cloud sync, no user accounts, and no server-side database.

Camera

The camera is used to scan PSA graded card labels and capture card photos. Camera frames are processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No camera data is transmitted to any server. Camera access requires your explicit permission.

Data Deletion

Go to Settings > Delete All Data to erase all local data, including card records, images, auth tokens, and preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes all data.

Data already sent to eBay (published listings, uploaded images) persists on eBay's servers per their retention policies. Deleting data in Slabr does not remove eBay listings.

Children's Privacy

Slabr does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not require account creation and stores no data on external servers.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as new features are added. Material changes will be communicated via app update release notes.
