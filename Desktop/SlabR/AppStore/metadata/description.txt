Slabr turns your PSA-graded sports cards into eBay listings in seconds.

Scan a slab. Confirm the details. Post to eBay. That's it.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Import your PSA scan from your camera roll — or point your camera at any card
2. Slabr reads the cert number, looks up every detail from PSA, and auto-fills your listing
3. Set your price, confirm the category, and publish directly to eBay

BUILT FOR SELLERS WHO MOVE VOLUME
• Auto-generated eBay titles optimized for search
• Photo uploads included — your scan becomes the listing image
• Category auto-detection via eBay's taxonomy
• Business policy integration applied automatically

RAW CARDS TOO
Point your camera at any ungraded card — select condition, set price, list it.

TRACK YOUR INVENTORY
• Dashboard with total value, active listings, and recent activity
• Analytics with grade distribution, brand breakdown, and CSV export
